Upload Jane Sessions Report

Where to get this report in Jane

To upload your Jane Sessions CSV, follow these steps inside the Jane app:

1. Go to Reports → Sales → Sales by Staff.

2. Select the date range you want to export.

3. Click Export and choose CSV.

4. Save the file to your computer, then upload it using the panel on the right.

This CSV contains all staff sessions and is required for Clinic Dash Pro to generate operational and financial insights.
