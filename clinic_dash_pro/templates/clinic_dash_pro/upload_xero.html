Upload Xero Categorized Transactions

Where to get this report in Xero

To upload your Xero categorized transactions CSV:

1. Go to Accounting → Bank Accounts.

2. Select the bank account you want to export.

3. Click Bank Statements → Export.

4. Choose CSV and download the file.

This file contains categorized transactions used by Clinic Dash Pro to compute monthly operational totals.
